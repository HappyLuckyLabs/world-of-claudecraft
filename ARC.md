# ARC.md: The Journey

The main story arc, set by James 2026-08-04. The movie-familiar shape of the
hero's journey through generic fantasy stations. Names below follow PARODY.md:
every station is a public-domain trope with our own name: the *shape* is the
homage, never the words. The GDD's mirrored Good/Evil progressions collapse
into ONE shared journey for v1; Alignment colors dialogue, quest framing, and
the ending inclination, not the route.

## Rulings & recommendations

- **The river beat**: ring-from-the-water is the right level of deep-cut: it
  reads as fantasy, lands for people who know. Passes the PARODY.md dial at
  level 1.
- **Highwatch as open PvP**: recommend **v1 = PvE siege** (scheduled siege
  events + raid; the city feels contested without PvP engineering). Open
  faction PvP in the siege zone is the existing "[Later] Faction PvP" milestone
  (#14): the engine is factionless today and that's the single biggest
  engineering item. Don't gate the journey on it.
- **Raids and quests at each city**: each city station gets a quest hub + one
  instanced piece (elven raid, the Delves, the Highwatch siege raid). The
  engine's five dungeons + delves + raid finale give us the mechanical
  skeletons to re-home.
- **The Evil side is post-v1** (James, 2026-08-04): the journey IS the Good
  team's arc. Shadowmire and everything Shadow-flavored (#5) comes after the
  journey ships; Alignment selection ships with it.
- **Pacing**: stations 1to2 are one sitting; 3 is the first "venture" (travel,
  danger, arrival); 4to6 are the meat (each a zone's worth of quests + its
  instance); 7 is the finale. Level 20 cap matches the engine.
